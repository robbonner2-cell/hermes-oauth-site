Privacy policy

Hermes Workspace is a personal automation tool operated by a single individual for their own account. This policy describes how it handles Google user data.

What is accessed

With the account owner's consent, the tool uses Google OAuth scopes for Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and Google Sheets. It reads and writes only the data belonging to the account that granted consent: messages, calendar events, files, and spreadsheet values.

How data is used

Data is used solely to perform the tasks the owner requests — reading mail to summarise or triage it, creating and reading calendar events, reading and writing files and spreadsheets. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, or model training, and no data is sold.

Where data is stored

Authentication tokens and any cached content are stored on the owner's own private server, under the owner's control. Data is not transferred to any third party other than the Google APIs needed to carry out the requested operation.

Retention and deletion

Cached content is retained only as long as needed for the requested task. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates the tool's tokens.

Limited use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions can be directed to the developer contact address configured on this application's OAuth consent screen in the Google Cloud console.
