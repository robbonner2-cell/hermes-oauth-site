Hermes Workspace

Hermes Workspace is a private, single-operator automation tool. It runs on its owner's own server and connects to that same owner's Google account through the Google APIs, with the owner's explicit consent, to read and write the owner's own mail, calendar, drive files and spreadsheets.

It is not a public service, has no sign-up, and is not offered to any third party.
